# AGH Welfare Election — visual system

Hybrid of UI/UX Pro Max recommendations:

- Product pattern: accessible civic / government (high contrast, skip link, 44px targets, visible focus, reduced motion).
- Typography: Luxury Serif — Cormorant headings, Montserrat body.
- Color structure: luxury dark + gold, mapped onto hospital forest rather than navy or wedding pink.

Do not use low-contrast champagne text for body copy. Gold is for rules, kickers, and large accents only.
